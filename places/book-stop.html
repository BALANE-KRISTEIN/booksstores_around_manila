The Book Stop Project: A Pop-up Literary Haven

Location: Plaza Roma, Intramuros, Philippines

About the Place

The Book Stop Project is a unique pop-up library located in Plaza Roma, Intramuros, Manila. It is a place where people can discover books and enjoy reading while exploring one of the most historic areas in Manila.

Unlike an ordinary bookstore, The Book Stop Project encourages people to exchange, swap, take, and leave books. This makes the place more than just a location to find books because it also creates an opportunity for people to share their love for reading with others.

The project provides an interesting experience for students, tourists, book lovers, and anyone who wants to discover something new while visiting Intramuros.

Place Details

Location Plaza Roma, Intramuros, Manila, Philippines
Type Pop-up Library and Book Exchange
Best For Students, readers, book lovers, and tourists
Price Free / Book Exchange
Best Time to Visit Daytime

Why I Recommend The Book Stop Project

I recommend The Book Stop Project because it offers a different experience from a traditional bookstore. Instead of simply purchasing books, visitors can participate in a community where books are shared and exchanged.

It is also a great place to visit for people who enjoy reading and exploring historical locations. Combining books with the atmosphere of Intramuros makes the experience more interesting and memorable.
