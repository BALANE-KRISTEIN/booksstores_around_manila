Books from Underground: The Ultimate Hidden Gem

Location: Lagusnilad Underpass, Padre Burgos Avenue, Manila

About the Place

Books from Underground is a unique bookstore located in the Lagusnilad Underpass near Manila City Hall. Unlike the usual bookstores found inside shopping malls, this bookstore can be found in a pedestrian underpass.

The bookstore offers a variety of books, including historical and academic materials. Because of its location near schools and universities, it can be a useful place for students who are looking for books for their studies.

Its unusual location makes Books from Underground an interesting hidden gem for people who enjoy discovering bookstores in unexpected places around Manila.

Why I Recommend Books from Underground

I recommend Books from Underground because it offers a different bookstore experience. Its location in an underpass makes it interesting and unique compared with the more common bookstores found in malls.

It is also worth visiting for students and readers who are interested in historical and academic books. If you enjoy discovering hidden places around Manila, this bookstore is a place worth checking out.
